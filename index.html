Explain quantum computing in simple terms for a 10-year-old.

Luna

Imagine you have a magic coin. While it's spinning in the air, it's not heads or tails... it's kind of *both* at the same time! You only find out which one it is when it lands.

Normal computers are like light switches: they are either ON (1) or OFF (0).

Quantum computers are like those magic coins. Their "qubits" can be ON, OFF, or both at the same time! This lets them try out millions of possibilities all at once, making them super powerful for solving really, really hard problems.
